Bunnys Caption

Auto captions for Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects

Made by Bunnys Framelab · Instagram · Website

Bunnys Caption listens to your video, writes the captions and places them on your Premiere timeline, each caption as its own editable clip, styled and animated. Everything runs on your own PC: no internet, no account, no upload.

Which model, when and why

A model is the "brain" that turns speech into text. Bigger models make fewer mistakes, but are slower and use more disk space. You only need the model for your language.

English · Recommended (Base)60 MBEnglish videos. The best balance, start here.

In the Generate tab, pick the language yourself. "Any language" can mistake Bangla for Hindi.

How it works

  1. The panel exports the sequence audio.
  2. The voice detector cuts the audio at pauses.
  3. The speech engine (whisper.cpp) and your model write the text, with the time of every word.
  4. For Bangla and Hindi, a word list fixes common spelling mistakes.
  5. The text is split into short captions using the style's rules.
  6. Each caption lands on the timeline as a Bunnys Caption graphic with the style applied.
